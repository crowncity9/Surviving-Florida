# Release Notes

## v1.0.0 — The Palm Haven Edition

**Public release date:** August 26, 2026

This is the first complete public release of the **Surviving Florida** official website and reader experience by RRF.

### Highlights

- Published the official Surviving Florida website through GitHub Pages.
- Released the complete Chapters 1–21 online reader.
- Added chapter selection, previous/next controls, reading progress, adjustable text size, reading themes, and saved reader preferences.
- Added the official book page with synopsis, chapter library, Amazon purchase link, and downloads.
- Added searchable character archives covering 56 characters.
- Added six illustrated Palm Haven location profiles.
- Added a complete publishing center for reading, buying, downloading, and sharing.
- Added PDF and Microsoft Word editions of the complete manuscript.
- Added the official character roster PDF.
- Added launch banner, square, story, and QR promotional graphics.
- Added social sharing metadata and mobile-responsive navigation.

### Recommended reader path

1. Open the [official website](https://crowncity9.github.io/Surviving-Florida/).
2. Begin with [Chapter One](https://crowncity9.github.io/Surviving-Florida/read.html#chapter-1).
3. Use [character profiles](https://crowncity9.github.io/Surviving-Florida/characters.html) when you want background on a character or faction.
4. Explore the [Palm Haven locations](https://crowncity9.github.io/Surviving-Florida/locations.html) as the story expands.
5. Visit the [publishing center](https://crowncity9.github.io/Surviving-Florida/publishing.html) to buy or download the book.

### Available files

- Complete 21-chapter manuscript in PDF
- Complete 21-chapter manuscript in Microsoft Word
- Official character roster in PDF
- Promotional launch artwork

### Notes

- Reading progress and display preferences are stored locally on each reader’s device.
- The Microsoft Word manuscript is provided as a publishing file.
- The official Kindle edition is available through the Amazon link on the book and publishing pages.
- This release contains story material through Chapter 21.
