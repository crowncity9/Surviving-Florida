SURVIVING
FLORIDA

Kareem “Reck” Dawson rises from Sunridge Gardens carrying a crown built from loyalty, grief and unfinished family business. When Khaos rebuilds the Haven Boyz and two crews unite, their fight expands beyond rival blocks into a network of cartel routes, corrupt police, prisons, banks, politicians and the billionaires profiting from every crisis.

What begins at the Back Court becomes a battle over who owns Palm Haven—and whether the next generation can protect its people without inheriting every mistake that created the war.
